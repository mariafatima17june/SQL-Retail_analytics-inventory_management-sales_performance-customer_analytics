****Online Bookstore Analytics | PostgreSQL**

**SQL | Retail Analytics | Inventory Management | Sales Performance | Customer Analytics**

**Project Overview****
Developed a PostgreSQL-based analytics project to examine key retail business areas, including product catalogue management, inventory availability, sales performance and customer purchasing behaviour.
Designed a relational database connecting Books, Customers and Orders to support business analysis through structured SQL queries and data-driven reporting.


**Business Objectives**
•	Analyse product catalogue distribution and pricing across genres.
•	Identify zero-stock and low-stock books to support inventory monitoring.
•	Evaluate monthly revenue trends and month-over-month growth.
•	Identify high-performing genres, books and authors.
•	Analyse customer purchase frequency and repeat-purchase behaviour.
•	Identify customers and products with no recorded transactions.

**Key Findings**
**Catalogue & Inventory Analysis**
•	Analysed 500 books across 7 genres.
•	Identified 5 zero-stock titles.
•	Found 64 books with 10 or fewer units in stock.
•	Identified 150 books with 30 or fewer units in stock.
•	Evaluated average pricing and genre-wise catalogue distribution.

**Sales & Customer Analytics**
Developed SQL queries to analyse monthly revenue, revenue growth, genre-wise sales, top-selling books, author performance, customer purchase frequency and customer spending rankings.
Technical Skills & SQL Concepts
•	Database: PostgreSQL, relational database design, primary and foreign keys.
•	SQL: SELECT, WHERE, GROUP BY, HAVING, ORDER BY, aggregate functions.
•	Joins: INNER JOIN, LEFT JOIN.
•	Advanced SQL: CASE WHEN, CTEs, subqueries and window functions.
•	Analytical Functions: DATE_TRUNC, LAG, DENSE_RANK, ROW_NUMBER.


**Business Value**
The project demonstrates how SQL can support retail decision-making by identifying inventory risks, monitoring sales performance, evaluating product demand and understanding customer purchasing behaviour.
The analysis translates business questions into structured SQL reporting and actionable recommendations for inventory planning, sales optimisation and customer retention.
